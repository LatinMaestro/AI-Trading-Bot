# Australian tax and record requirements

The application is not tax advice. A registered tax professional should review the treatment before live activation and at tax time.

The Australian Taxation Office states that records are required for each crypto asset and every transaction, including dates, purpose/counterparty, exchange records and Australian-dollar value at the time.

The system therefore records:

- UTC timestamp and Australian local date;
- asset and quantity;
- AUD market value and valuation source;
- order, fill, fee and spread details;
- acquisition and disposal linkage;
- exchange identifiers;
- strategy and software version;
- transfers and adjustments entered by the user;
- records needed to reproduce gains, losses, income and expenses.

Records must be exportable in a human-readable CSV and a machine-readable format. Raw exchange records are retained where provider terms and storage policy permit.

Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/investments-and-assets/crypto-asset-investments/keeping-crypto-records
